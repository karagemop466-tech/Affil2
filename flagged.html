Flagged for Review

Everything this site could not verify, every place a listing contradicts itself, and every policy decision that shaped the copy. None of these stop the site going live; all of them are visible here with a source link so you can check them yourself.

Read this page in this order

  1. Conflicts — places where a listing says two different things (8 active contradictions). These are the ones most likely to affect a buying decision.
  2. Policy — things deliberately absent for compliance reasons: prices, ratings, product photography, hands-on claims.
  3. Unverified / Pending test — values we could not confirm from public records or Associates Central, left blank rather than guessed.
  4. Open / Corrected — operational gaps, redirect snapshots, and earlier mistakes fixed in the register.

How to request a change

Note the flag ID, say what is wrong, and cite the official page that shows otherwise. Corrections are dated and the flag is kept rather than deleted, so the record shows what changed and when. If a listing has changed since 2026-11-20, that is a correction, not an error — say so and it gets re-read.
