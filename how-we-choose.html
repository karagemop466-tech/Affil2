How we choose, and what we will not claim

This site exists to help you pick one appliance, not to rank the market. That means saying plainly where the information comes from, what we have not done, and what we refuse to print.

What "verified" means here

It means one person opened the official Amazon listing for the product on 2026-11-20 and read the value off the page. Each product page carries the date, and every specification row links to the listing it came from. It does not mean we tested the product, and it does not mean the listing is correct — only that we read what it says and recorded it accurately.

What we have not done

  • We have not cooked with any of these. There is no hands-on test, no side-by-side taste trial, and no measured performance. Nothing on this site implies otherwise (FLG-032).
  • We have not seen the certificates. NIST and NSF appear on the listings. We have not been given the documents or an independent test report (FLG-035).
  • We have not confirmed the submitted short links. Their compliance status is unresolved, so we do not call them valid or invalid (FLG-003). The links we publish are ordinary tagged amazon.com URLs.

What we will not print

  • Prices and availability — Amazon's Program Policies only allow these when Amazon serves the link or the data comes from their API. Ours does not, so the number stays on Amazon (S02).
  • Star ratings and review counts — they summarise other people's kitchens, and quoting them invites the reader to treat popularity as suitability.
  • A commission rate or a revenue forecast — neither can be verified from outside the account, so neither is published.
  • A "best" verdict without your counter measurements and household size. We give you the trade-off instead.

How conflicts are handled

When a listing contradicts itself, we print both figures in the specification table and give the contradiction an ID on the Flagged for Review page. There are currently several: preset counts, accessory counts, top temperature, wattage, capacity units and warranty length. We do not average them, pick the flattering one, or quietly drop the awkward one.

How the money works

Outbound product links carry our Amazon Associate tag and are marked (paid link) next to the button, with rel="sponsored nofollow" on the anchor, per Google's guidance (S04). The disclosure statement sits under the masthead on every page and repeats in the footer, per Amazon's Operating Agreement Section 5 (S01) and FTC guidance (S03). Untagged listing links are published alongside so you can check our work without the tag.

How to tell us we are wrong

Every discrepancy, blank and open question is listed on the Flagged for Review page with a source link. Corrections are dated. If a listing has changed since 2026-11-20, that page is the place to say so.
