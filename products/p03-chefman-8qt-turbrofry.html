What is the maximum temperature?

Conflicting: the About this item bullet and Max Temperature Setting spec field say 400 °F, while the listing title advertises a 450 °F Hi-Fry option. Both are published; neither is chosen. Flagged as FLG-018.

How many watts?

Conflicting: the Features & Specs Output Wattage and Wattage fields say 1700 watts, while the About this item bullet says 1000 watts / 120 volts. Both are shown here and flagged as FLG-017.

Is the basket dishwasher safe?

Yes, the listing says the non-stick basket and flat frying tray are both top-rack dishwasher safe.

Does it come with a warranty?
