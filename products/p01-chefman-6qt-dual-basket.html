Quick answers

What is the total capacity?

6 quarts, split into two independent 3-quart baskets, according to the listing title and bullets. The product-details Measurements field says 6 liters, which is about 6.3 US quarts. Flagged as FLG-034.

Does it have a preheat function?

The listing does not state a preheat step. Presets and a +2 minute button are listed. Do not assume preheat.

What is the maximum temperature?

450 °F, per the listing's About this item bullets and Max Temperature Setting field.

Is it dishwasher safe?

The listing says the removable non-stick coated baskets are dishwasher safe.
