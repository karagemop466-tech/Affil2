Quick answers

How long does the battery last?

The current listing says up to 50 hours on a full charge, and that is what we publish. An earlier reading of the product description said 70 hours. Both figures come from the same listing at different times, so treat 50 hours as the present claim rather than a measured run time.

How many probes come in the box?

Three 4.75-inch probes, plus the hub (model CQ60-3C-Z1-E). Note the listing also sells 1, 2 and 4 probe options — check the size selector before buying.

Does it work without WiFi?

The listing says Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Bluetooth pairing is described in the bullets, but the marketing is built around Wi-Fi unlimited range. Do not assume full range without Wi-Fi.

Is it dishwasher safe, and what is the warranty?

The listing says the probes are waterproof and dishwasher safe (hub care is unstated). On warranty, About this item states a two-year assurance while Item details Warranty Description says 3 Year (,).

Doneness targets come from the USDA, not from us

This is a thermometer, so the numbers that matter are food-safety numbers. We do not restate them from memory. The USDA FSIS Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (source S22, checked 2026-11-20) gives these minimums, as measured with a food thermometer:

FoodMinimum internal temperatureSource
Poultry — breasts, whole bird, legs, thighs, wings, ground poultry, giblets, stuffing165 °F (73.9 °C)USDA chart (S22)
Ground meats160 °F (71.1 °C)USDA chart (S22)
Beef, pork, veal and lamb steaks, chops and roasts145 °F (62.8 °C) with a rest of at least 3 minutesUSDA chart (S22)
