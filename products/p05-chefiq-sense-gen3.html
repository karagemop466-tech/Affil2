Quick answers

How long does the battery last?

The About this item bullet says up to 50 hours on a full charge. The prior pass recorded 70 hours from a different part of the page that no longer shows that figure. Both are flagged; treat 50 hours as the current listing claim. See FLG-008.

How many probes come in the box?

Three, plus the hub. Note the listing also sells 1, 2 and 4 probe versions — check the size selector before buying.

Does it work without WiFi?

The listing says WiFi and Bluetooth. Bluetooth pairing is described in the bullets, but the marketing is built around WiFi range. Do not assume full range without WiFi.

Is it dishwasher safe?

The listing says the probes are waterproof and dishwasher safe. The hub's care instructions are not stated.
