Quick answers

How long does the battery last?

The About this item bullet says up to 50 hours on a full charge. An earlier capture recorded 70 hours from Product Description text not visible in current fetched HTML. Both are flagged; treat 50 hours as the current listing claim. See FLG-008.

How many probes come in the box?

Three 4.75-inch probes, plus the hub (model CQ60-3C-Z1-E). Note the listing also sells 1, 2 and 4 probe options — check the size selector before buying.

Does it work without WiFi?

The listing says Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Bluetooth pairing is described in the bullets, but the marketing is built around Wi-Fi unlimited range. Do not assume full range without Wi-Fi.

Is it dishwasher safe, and what is the warranty?

The listing says the probes are waterproof and dishwasher safe (hub care is unstated). On warranty, About this item states a two-year assurance while Item details Warranty Description says 3 Year (FLG-038, FLG-019).

Doneness targets come from the USDA, not from us

This is a thermometer, so the numbers that matter are food-safety numbers. We do not restate them from memory. The USDA FSIS Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (source S22, checked 2026-11-20) gives these minimums, as measured with a food thermometer:

USDA FSIS — Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (S22)
FoodMinimum internal temperature
Poultry — breasts, whole bird, legs, thighs, wings, ground poultry, giblets, stuffing165 °F (73.9 °C)
Ground meats160 °F (71.1 °C)
Beef, pork, veal and lamb steaks, chops and roasts145 °F (62.8 °C) with a rest of at least 3 minutes
Fish and shellfish145 °F (62.8 °C)
Leftovers and casseroles165 °F (73.9 °C)
