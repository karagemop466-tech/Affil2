Doneness targets come from the USDA, not from us

This is a thermometer, so the numbers that matter are food-safety numbers. We do not restate them from memory. The USDA FSIS Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (source S22, checked 2026-11-20) gives these minimums, as measured with a food thermometer:

USDA FSIS — Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (S22)
FoodMinimum internal temperature
Poultry — breasts, whole bird, legs, thighs, wings, ground poultry, giblets, stuffing165 °F (73.9 °C)
Ground meats160 °F (71.1 °C)
Beef, pork, veal and lamb steaks, chops and roasts145 °F (62.8 °C) with a rest of at least 3 minutes
Fish and shellfish145 °F (62.8 °C)
Leftovers and casseroles165 °F (73.9 °C)

Read the chart itself before relying on any number here: fsis.usda.gov. Registered as S22 in the source register.
