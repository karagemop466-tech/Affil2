Suggestions

Every idea for this site lands here before anything is built. Read the proposal, the reason it matters and what it would cost, then record a decision: approve, defer or reject. Nothing on this page changes the site by itself. Your decisions are stored in this browser and export as CSV.

How to use this page in three minutes

  1. Read the Status column first. Shipped means it is already live and the check column tells you where to look.
  2. Anything marked Blocked or Not started needs a decision or a credential from the owner. Those are the rows where a click changes something.
  3. Click Approve, Defer or Reject. The tally under the table updates, and the export gives you a dated list to work from.

What we will not do without a new decision

  • Print prices or star ratings copied from a listing.
  • Call any product “best” without evidence.
  • Auto-publish generated content without passing the build gate.
  • Merge the intended and observed ASIN fields.
  • Scale outbound links before the one-per-product test passes.
