Decision Center

Every proposal for this site lands here first. Nothing below is auto-applied. Read the proposal, its evidence, and its cost, then rank it. Your ranking is stored in your browser and can be exported as CSV, so you can review the shortlist before anything changes.

How to use this page in three minutes

  1. Read the Status column first. Anything marked Shipped is already live — the verification note tells you where to check it.
  2. Anything marked Blocked or Not started needs a decision or a credential from you. Rank those against each other.
  3. Use the arrows to order the shortlist, then export the ranking. Nothing is built from this page without an explicit choice recorded in the worksheet at the bottom.
