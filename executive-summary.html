3. What is verified

  • Every product title, dimension, weight (where stated), wattage, temperature range and capacity on this site was read from the official listing and its Product information tables on 2026-11-20. Deep chunk inspection verified previously blank weights (P-01 13.3 lb, P-03 12.8 lb), models (P-02 4-00675-02X-VN, P-04 HT07, P-05 CQ60-3C-Z1-E), temperatures (P-02 75–500 °F, P-04 572 °F), P-02 power (1700 W), and probe lengths (P-04 6.5 in, P-05 4.75 in) (FLG-040).
  • All official policy sources — Operating Agreement Section 5 (S01), Program Policies Participation Requirements §2(a)/(b), §5, §6(t)/(v)/(w)/(y), IP License §1, §2(e)/(h)/(i)/(k), §4 Agent Terms, Commission Income Statement §2(d)/(e)/(g) (S02), FTC disclosure guidance (S03, S23), Google outbound-link qualification (S04), and Creators API / PA-API 5 notices (S20, S21) — are quoted in the source register.
  • Food-safety temperatures and thermometer calibration guidance come from USDA FSIS charts (S22, S24), not from product listings.
  • All five short links were fetched and their redirect targets observed separately from intent (including B0c8moW59 resolving to B0DHZTCNR5 on re-test while redirect instability stays under FLG-011).

4. What is not verified, and is not pretended to be

  • Intent. Only the owner knows which ASIN each link was meant to point at (FLG-001).
  • The tracking ID. Whether shopnow047c-20 is live is visible only inside Associates Central (FLG-002).
  • The short-link format. Its compliance status is unresolved, so the site uses tagged amazon.com/dp/ URLs and does not call the short links invalid (FLG-003).
  • The link test. No tagged link has been clicked inside the enrolled account, so every product page still says PENDING TEST (FLG-004).
  • P-02 weight and warranty terms. Absent from the live listing HTML, so left blank (FLG-022); an unverified Emeril weight figure from earlier editorial prose was removed (FLG-039).
  • Certification. NIST, NSF, and cETL appear on the listings. No certificate or test report has been seen (FLG-035).
  • Hands-on experience. None. Nothing on this site implies it was tested (FLG-032).

6. What would change the numbers

Two credentials, hosting for the collector, and one domain decision. Creators API access (S20, S21) would make legal price and product-photo display possible (SG-13, SG-15, SG-31), which is the largest lever on conversion. Search Console verification would make query data available (SG-10, SG-29, SG-32), which is the only way to choose the next guide from evidence. Hosting the shipped self-hosted analytics endpoint (SG-25, FLG-010) enables cross-visitor event counts without cookies, and a custom domain (SG-14, FLG-006) strengthens brand and indexation. Everything else on the Decision Center is shipped or blocked on owner account verification.

7. Honest position on revenue

No commission rate, click count, conversion rate or revenue figure is presented here, because none can be obtained from an authorised first-party report yet. The campaign ledger is empty by design and its schema is defined. When the tracking report produces a row, that row goes in with a date and a source. Until then the honest number is zero, and it is stated as zero rather than dressed up.
