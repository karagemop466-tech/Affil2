A static publication site deployable straight to GitHub Pages: a front page that leads with products, two category pages, five product pages, eight question-shaped guides, a comparison table aligned by field, an on-site search that actually works, JSON-LD on every page, a sitemap, an RSS feed, and a disclosure strip under the masthead on every page. Alongside it sit the internal records: the Decision Center, the Open Questions register, the flag register, the source register, and an intentionally empty campaign ledger.

3. What is verified

  • Every product title, dimension, weight, wattage, temperature range and capacity on this site was read from the official listing on 2026-11-20.
  • The four policy sources — Operating Agreement Section 5, Program Policies 2(a) and 2(b), FTC disclosure guidance and Google's outbound-link guidance — are quoted with exact wording in the source register.
  • Food-safety temperatures come from the USDA FSIS chart, not from a product listing.
  • All five short links were fetched and their destinations recorded separately from intent.

4. What is not verified, and is not pretended to be

  • Intent. Only the owner knows which ASIN each link was meant to point at (FLG-001).
  • The tracking ID. Whether shopnow047c-20 is live is visible only inside Associates Central (FLG-002).
  • The short-link format. Its compliance status is unresolved, so the site uses tagged amazon.com/dp/ URLs and does not call the short links invalid (FLG-003).
  • The link test. No tagged link has been clicked inside the enrolled account, so every product page still says PENDING TEST (FLG-004).
  • Certification. NIST and NSF appear on the listings. No certificate or test report has been seen (FLG-035).
  • Hands-on experience. None. Nothing on this site implies it was tested (FLG-032).

6. What would change the numbers

Two credentials, and one decision. Product Advertising API access would make legal price display possible (SG-13), which is the largest single lever on conversion. Search Console verification would make query data available (SG-10), which is the only way to choose the next guide from evidence rather than instinct. A custom domain decision (SG-14, FLG-006) would strengthen brand and indexation. Everything else on the Decision Center is either shipped or cosmetic.

7. Honest position on revenue

No commission rate, click count, conversion rate or revenue figure is presented here, because none can be obtained from an authorised first-party report yet. The campaign ledger is empty by design and its schema is defined. When the tracking report produces a row, that row goes in with a date and a source. Until then the honest number is zero, and it is stated as zero rather than dressed up.
