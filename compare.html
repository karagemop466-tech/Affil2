Two at a time

How to read this table

  1. Filter by form first. Two baskets, one basket, or an oven — that decision eliminates more options than any spec.
  2. Measure before you compare. Write down your counter depth and width, then strike out anything that does not fit with clearance.
  3. Treat the catch column as the tie-breaker. Every product has one; pick the one you can live with.

Where a listing contradicts itself, the conflicting figures both appear in the cell and the flag ID is on the Flagged for Review page.

The Price and Star rating rows say "not shown, by policy" rather than "not available": the official listings do print a price, a star rating, a review count and a best-sellers rank, and those fields were visible when each page was read on 2026-11-20. They are excluded here on purpose — Amazon's Program Policies only permit a site to show price and availability when Amazon serves the link or the data comes through the Product Advertising / Creators API, and this site does neither (FLG-005). Look at the listing for the current numbers; they change, and the table below would go stale within days if we copied them.
