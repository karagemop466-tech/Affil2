Disclosure, Privacy & Analytics

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Every page carries this statement above the fold (under the masthead) and again in the footer. Every outbound Amazon link is labelled (paid link) next to the button and carries rel="sponsored nofollow", per Google guidance (S04) and FTC guidance (S03): clear, conspicuous, and near the link.

What we do not show

Prices and availability are not displayed (Program Policies §2(b): only via Amazon-served links or Creators/PA API). Customer ratings and reviews are not copied. “Best” claims are not made without evidence.

Privacy-preserving measurement

Five events are defined: page_view, outbound_click, guide_read, search, suggestion_rank. No cookies, no third-party trackers, no fingerprinting, no IP address, no user agent. A search is recorded as a length and a result count, never as the words you typed.

The reader-visible counter on the Campaign Ledger page stores counts only in your own browser (localStorage) and never leaves your device. Separately, this repository ships a self-hosted collector that would record the same five events server-side: functions/api/collect.js as a Cloudflare Pages Function, and analytics/server.js as a dependency-free Node server. Until one of them is deployed and the site is pointed at it, the collector sends nothing at all — the live site collects no server-side data, and server-side conversion data is blank by design. See Measurement for the event schema and the deployment steps, and Flagged for Review (FLG-033).

Product-intent records

For each submitted link we store intended ASIN (blank until owner confirms), observed redirect target, official listing URL, confirmation date, and source — in separate fields. See any product page.
