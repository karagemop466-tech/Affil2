Disclosure, privacy and measurement

This page explains the words at the top of every page, what we are not allowed to show, and exactly what happens to anything you do here.

The affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Links marked (paid link) are Amazon affiliate links — if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

The wording is the statement Amazon's Operating Agreement Section 5 requires, and it appears on every page directly under the masthead, then again in the footer. FTC guidance on disclosures says a disclosure placed only on an about page or at the end of a page is likely to be missed, so it appears before the first product link rather than after it.

Every outbound product link is labelled (paid link) next to the button and carries rel="sponsored nofollow", per Google's guidance on qualifying paid links. Where a product page offers a second link to the same listing without our tag, it is labelled as the untagged listing so you can verify the product yourself without sending us a click.

What we are not allowed to show

Amazon's Program Policies section 2(b) lets a site show prices and availability only if Amazon serves the link the price appears in, or if the data comes through the Product Advertising or Creators API. This site does neither, so no price, availability, star rating, review count or best-seller rank is displayed anywhere. Where those values were visible on a listing when we read it, they were recorded in the working data and deliberately excluded from the pages. That is why the price column on the comparison table says "not shown, by policy" rather than being empty.

What we measure

The five events below are counted locally in this browser. The measurement record stores aggregate counts only; it does not retain raw search terms, a page-by-page event timeline, timestamps, referrers, IP addresses, or decision text.

EventFires whenWhat is counted
page_viewA page loadsOne aggregate increment; no page path is attached
outbound_clickA tagged product link is clicked An aggregate total and product ID only
guide_readA guide page loadsA guide-load count, not proof that its text was read
searchA non-empty search is submitted and evaluated One aggregate increment; the query and result text are not stored
suggestion_rankA proposal is moved in the Decision Center One aggregate increment; the chosen order is stored separately in this browser

These counters are not site-wide analytics and are not sent to us. They persist in this browser's local storage until the site's data is cleared, and can be exported as counts: .

The suggestion ranking and decision worksheet are separate local browser data. The worksheet may contain text you choose to enter; it is not transmitted, and you can export or clear it from the Decision Center. No server-side analytics endpoint is deployed. GitHub Pages is a static host; its host documentation says visitor IP addresses are logged for security purposes. That host-level logging is separate from the counters described here.

With JavaScript enabled, the built-in header search carries a one-time query in a URL fragment, which browsers do not send in the HTTP request, and clears it after the results load. A legacy URL containing ?q= or the no-JavaScript form fallback may include the query in the request URL and ordinary host logs. Do not include sensitive personal information in a search.

Product-intent records

For each of the five submitted links, the intended ASIN and the observed redirect target are stored in separate fields on the product page and are never merged. The observed target is a dated observation, not confirmation of the owner's intended product or a guarantee of a stable redirect. Each product page links to the direct listing used as its reference.

Cookies, and what we do not use

  • No advertising cookies, retargeting, social pixels, or fingerprinting.
  • No third-party analytics script, pixel, or tag manager.
  • No account, login, or email capture on this site.
  • Clicking an Amazon link leaves this site; Amazon's privacy practices then apply.
