Disclosure, privacy and measurement

This page explains the words at the top of every page, what we are not allowed to show, and exactly what happens to anything you do here.

The affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Links marked (paid link) are Amazon affiliate links — if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

The wording is the statement Amazon's Operating Agreement Section 5 requires, and it appears on every page directly under the masthead, then again in the footer. FTC guidance on disclosures says a disclosure placed only on an about page or at the end of a page is likely to be missed, so it appears before the first product link rather than after it.

Every outbound product link is labelled (paid link) next to the button and carries rel="sponsored nofollow", per Google's guidance on qualifying paid links. Where a product page offers a second link to the same listing without our tag, it is labelled as the untagged listing so you can verify the product yourself without sending us a click.

What we are not allowed to show

Amazon's Program Policies section 2(b) lets a site show prices and availability only if Amazon serves the link the price appears in, or if the data comes through the Product Advertising or Creators API. This site does neither, so no price, availability, star rating, review count or best-seller rank is displayed anywhere. Where those values were visible on a listing when we read it, they were recorded in the working data and deliberately excluded from the pages. That is why the price column on the comparison table says "not shown, by policy" rather than being empty.

What we measure

Five events, defined once and used consistently:

EventFires whenUsed for
page_viewAny page loadsWhich pages are reached at all
outbound_clickA tagged Amazon link is clicked Which product a reader actually leaves for
guide_readA guide page loadsWhether guides get read, not just listed
searchA search query returns results What readers look for and do not find
suggestion_rankA proposal is reordered on the Decision Center Which direction is actually preferred

All five are counted in your browser's local storage and nothing else. No cookie is set, no request carries your data to a third party, and no fingerprinting is used. There is no analytics script, no pixel and no tag manager on this site. A self-hosted endpoint could replace local storage later, and the event names are fixed so that swap would not require editing any page — but until that endpoint exists, nothing leaves your device.

Product-intent records

For each of the five submitted links, the intended ASIN and the observed redirect target are stored in separate fields on the product page and are never merged. Merging them would assert an intention only the owner can confirm. Each product page also carries the date the values were read and a link to the official listing.

Cookies, and what we do not use

  • No advertising cookies, no retargeting, no social pixels.
  • No fonts or scripts loaded from a third-party CDN — the page loads from this domain only.
  • No account, no login, no email capture on this site.
  • Clicking a product link takes you to Amazon, where Amazon's own privacy policy applies and Amazon's own cookies are set.
