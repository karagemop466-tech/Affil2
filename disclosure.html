Disclosure, Privacy & Analytics

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Every page carries this statement above the fold (under the masthead) and again in the footer. Every outbound Amazon link is labelled (paid link) next to the button and carries rel="sponsored nofollow", per Google guidance (S04) and FTC guidance (S03): clear, conspicuous, and near the link.

What we do not show

Prices and availability are not displayed (Program Policies §2(b): only via Amazon-served links or Creators/PA API). Customer ratings and reviews are not copied. “Best” claims are not made without evidence.

Privacy-preserving measurement

Five events are defined and nothing else is collected: page_view, outbound_click, guide_read, search, suggestion_rank. The measurement layer carries no cookies, no identifiers, no referrers, and never records what you type — a search event stores only a result count. Two modes:

  • Browser-local (current default): counts stay in your own browser (localStorage) and can be exported as CSV or cleared at any time. Nothing leaves your device.
  • Self-hosted endpoint (shipped, not yet deployed): analytics/server.py is a privacy-preserving collector that never reads IP addresses, writes no access logs, and stores only aggregate event counts. GitHub Pages cannot host server code, so deployment is a gated owner decision (Q-08). When it goes live, the switch is one line in assets/config.js and this page is updated.

Conversion data (orders, commissions) comes only from authorized first-party Amazon Associates reports into the campaign ledger — it is not guessed, forecast, or inferred. Until those reports exist, the ledger is empty by design.

Reader capture

Following this publication is RSS-first (feed.xml). Email capture is deliberately unconnected until an owner chooses a provider (Q-09 / SG-30) — the signup form says so honestly instead of pretending to work.

Product-intent records

For each submitted link we store intended ASIN (blank until owner confirms), observed redirect target, official listing URL, confirmation date, and source — in separate fields. See any product page.
