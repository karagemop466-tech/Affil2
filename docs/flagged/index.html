Flagged for review.

Irregularities, unverified items and open compliance questions. These do not block publication, and they are not quietly resolved. Each one states what is known, what is not, and what would close it.

Ordered by severity, then by ID. Severity reflects consequence if the item is left unaddressed.

FLG-001 High One supplied link resolves inconsistently
link.amazon/B0c8moW59 returned the ThermoMaven thermometer product page on the first fetch and the Amazon homepage on a second, separate fetch. Because the destination is not stable, no buyer-facing button points at this product, and its card carries a 'link under review' state.
Affects: ThermoMaven instant-read thermometer
Evidence: https://link.amazon/B0c8moW59 (not linked: destination under review)
 Re-generate the link in SiteStripe while signed in to the Associates account and record the resulting URL in intendedAsin and officialListingUrl.
FLG-003 High Compliance status of link.amazon short links is unresolved
Whether these exact short links satisfy the Special Link formatting and no-cloaking rules cannot be determined without (a) inspecting them while signed in to the Associates account and (b) confirming the tag is present in the redirect. Neither was possible here. This was explicitly flagged as unresolved in the brief and remains unresolved. No claim is made that the links are invalid.
Affects: All five products
Evidence: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
 Test one link per product inside the enrolled Associates workflow. Confirm destination and that the click appears in the tracking report before scaling to the other four.
FLG-004 High No tracking tag was observed in any resolved destination
None of the destination URLs observed during verification carried a visible tag= parameter. The Store ID shopnow047c-20 matches the documented US format (S-05), but whether it is attached to these links, and whether the account is enrolled, cannot be checked from outside Associates Central.
Affects: All five products
Evidence: https://www.amazon.com/b?node=51812349011
 Confirm enrollment and confirm the click appears in the Associates tracking report. Until then the site must not imply that a click on this site earns a commission.
FLG-007 High Certification and safety claims are unverified manufacturer claims
Product pages include manufacturer-stated certifications such as ETL and UL listings and stated accuracy figures. These are the brand's claims, not independently tested results. The site does not repeat them as verified facts and does not repeat them as endorsement.
Affects: All five products
Evidence: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
 Either omit these claims from buyer-facing copy or attribute them plainly to the manufacturer.
FLG-009 High Product images are not cleared for use
No product imagery is used on this site. The brand assets on this build are drawn, not photographed, and product cards intentionally use typography instead of photography. Amazon product imagery may only be used through Program Content under the IP License.
Affects: All pages
Evidence: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
 Either obtain images through the Creators API under the License, or keep the typographic treatment.
FLG-010 High Prices observed during verification are not published to buyers
Prices were observed while checking the links. They are held in this register as internal editorial context and are deliberately not shown on any buyer-facing page, because Amazon's rules restrict showing price and availability (Participation Requirements 2(b)).
Affects: All five products
Evidence: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
 Show price to buyers only if Amazon serves the link that contains it, or once Creators API access is in place and the data is presented under the License.
FLG-002 Medium Supplied short codes are not ASINs
All five supplied link.amazon codes are 9 characters. Amazon ASINs are 10 characters. Read as ASINs they are malformed, so they are treated as opaque short codes throughout. The 10-character product IDs they resolved to are recorded in a separate observed field, never merged with the intended-ASIN field.
Affects: All five products
Evidence: Recorded in verificationLog V-06.
 None required for the site. This is why intendedAsin and observedAsin are separate columns in the register.
FLG-005 Medium Product identity is observed, not confirmed
Product names, form factors and capacities on this site come from the product pages the short links resolved to. They are strong evidence but they are not a confirmation record signed off by the account holder. The confirmation-date and source fields are therefore deliberately empty.
Affects: All five products
Evidence: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
 For each product, record the intended ASIN, the official listing URL, the confirmation date and the source as four separate values.
FLG-008 Medium No product has been physically tested
The editorial desk has not handled, cooked with, or measured any of these appliances. Any first-person experience language would violate FTC guidance against describing experience with a product you have not tried.
Affects: All five products
Evidence: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers
 Keep all copy in second-person, spec-based language until a test protocol and at least one physical unit exist. This constraint is applied throughout the site.
FLG-011 Medium The research automation has no data source yet
Creators API access requires Associates enrollment plus at least 10 qualifying sales in the past 30 days (S-03). With no verified sales history, the product-research pipeline cannot be switched on. Any screen describing automated trend detection is a specification, not a working feature.
Affects: Research pipeline
Evidence: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/introduction
 Keep the ledger empty and clearly labelled until sales history exists. Do not present simulated data.
FLG-012 Medium No analytics provider and no email provider are connected
The five defined events are wired into the page, but there is no server endpoint to receive them and no email provider to accept an address. The capture form is therefore visibly disabled rather than silently discarding submissions.
Affects: Measurement and capture
Evidence: Not applicable. This is a statement of current configuration.
 Deploy a collector endpoint, then set analyticsEndpoint in assets/js/config.js. Connect an email provider before enabling the form.
FLG-015 Medium AI-assisted drafting is disclosed, not hidden
Copy on this site was drafted with an AI assistant from verified source material and a supplied product list. Amazon requires original content with significant commentary and analysis, and FTC guidance prohibits fabricated experience. The site therefore discloses the drafting method rather than presenting a fictional human byline.
Affects: All pages
Evidence: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
 If a named human editor takes over, update the byline and this flag together.
