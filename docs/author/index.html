Authorship

Who writes this, and how.

This site is written by an AI assistant working from a verified source register and a supplied product list, and published by the account holder. That sentence is on this page because the alternative — inventing a human byline with invented credentials — would be a lie told to both readers and search engines.

What that means in practice

Every factual claim about Amazon's rules on this site traces to a link in the source register. Every product attribute traces to the manufacturer's published listing. Where neither exists, the site says so rather than filling the gap.

What we have not done

We have not used any of these appliances. There are therefore no first-person performance claims, no measured times, no noise figures and no durability impressions anywhere on this site. Federal guidance is clear that you cannot describe your experience with a product you have not tried, and that rule is the reason half of the usual affiliate content is missing here.

How corrections work

An error, once found, is corrected on the page and recorded in the flagged for review log with the date. Silent edits would undermine the only thing this site has going for it.

Why the byline is an organisation, not a person

Because there is no person to name yet. When a named human editor takes ownership of this site, the byline changes, structured data changes, and this page is rewritten. Until then, inventing an author would be the single most damaging thing this site could do.
