Disclosure and editorial policy

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

That sentence appears under the masthead of every page on this site, and again in the footer. This page explains exactly what it means, where the links go, and what this site does not claim.

What the disclosure means

Outbound product links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something on Amazon, this site may earn a commission. The price you pay does not change.

Amazon requires Associates to state, clearly and prominently, that they earn from qualifying purchases. The required wording is reproduced exactly, without paraphrase, and it appears above the fold on every page rather than only on this one.

Where the links point

Buttons marked Paid link send you to Amazon. The exact destination for each product is recorded on that product's page, in a table that keeps the intended listing separate from the observed redirect. That separation is deliberate: one is what was intended, the other is what was actually observed.

What this site does not do

  • It does not show prices. Amazon restricts price display unless Amazon serves the link or the data arrives through its API.
  • It does not show customer ratings. The same restriction applies.
  • It does not claim hands-on testing. No product here has been tested.
  • It does not use Amazon trademarks in the site name, the domain or any identifier.
  • It does not run pop-ups, interstitials or exit overlays around affiliate links.
  • It does not place affiliate links in email or private messages.

Editorial independence

The list is short and it is not ranked. A product appearing here is not an assertion that it is the best at anything. Commission rates did not determine the order, because no commission data exists for this site yet and inventing it would defeat the purpose of the list.

Privacy, briefly

This site counts five aggregate events: page views, whether the disclosure strip was visible, outbound product clicks, guide read depth, and sign-up submissions. There are no cookies, no identifiers and no cross-site tracking. Your browser's Do Not Track setting is honoured. There is currently no analytics endpoint deployed, which means nothing is transmitted anywhere at all.

Contact

No contact address is published yet. That is an open item on the open questions page, not an oversight that has been quietly accepted.
