Rules of the ledger

Why it starts empty

  • No row is created from an estimate, a model, a projection, or a third-party dashboard screenshot.
  • Every row carries the date the report was pulled and the name of the report it came from.
  • A conversion rate is calculated, never stored. If the inputs change, the calculated figure must change with them.
  • Empty is a valid and honest state. A ledger that starts empty and fills from real reports is worth more than one that starts full of guesses.

Explicitly not presented

  • No conversion rate
  • No revenue forecast
  • No earnings per click
  • No benchmark from other sites

A fabricated benchmark is the fastest way to make a data page useless. This one stays empty until it can be true.
