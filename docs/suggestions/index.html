SUG-01 Publish one guide a week, chosen from Search Console queries rather than instinct
Six guides exist. A weekly cadence is sustainable for one writer and gives the sitemap new URLs to submit. Choosing the next question from measured queries is the only way to avoid writing into a void.
Cost: About 4 hours a week · Risk: Publishing ahead of measured demand wastes effort. Publishing too slowly gives Google too little to crawl.

The site currently cannot say anything about how these machines perform, because nobody has used them. A written protocol with published method turns that from a weakness into a differentiator.
Cost: One machine purchased per tested product, plus about 6 hours per test · Risk: Testing purchased units and then earning commission on them is allowed; the disclosure must be clear either way.

The order that avoids wasted work

  1. Close the link questions. Confirm enrollment, tag presence and the five intended ASINs. Everything commercial sits behind this.
  2. Write the privacy notice. Before any measurement or capture is switched on, not after.
  3. Deploy a collector. Then the ledger can start filling from reports instead of staying empty.
  4. Verify Search Console and submit the sitemap. Four weeks later, choose the next guide from queries.
  5. Only then consider a test protocol, imagery and a newsletter.

What choosing looks like

A proposal is ready to implement when its "depends on" column is satisfied and its status is not blocked. Nothing on this page has been silently implemented.
