Air fryers: two baskets, one big basket, or a whole oven

Three air fryers, three genuinely different answers to the same question: how do you cook? A dual-basket drawer, a single 8-quart basket, and a 26-quart countertop oven that air-fries.

Air Fryers · Chefman

Two baskets, two windows, one finish time

Two baskets you can watch through the windows, timed to finish together. The question is not whether it fries — it is whether two 3-quart baskets fit the way you actually cook.

Best for
Cooked-for-two households that want a main and a side done at once
The catch
