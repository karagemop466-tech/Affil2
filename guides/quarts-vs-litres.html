Six quarts or six litres? What to do when a listing gives two units

A listing that gives capacity in quarts in one field and litres in another is not necessarily wrong about either. Six US quarts is about 5.7 litres, and six litres is about 6.3 US quarts, so the two figures are close enough to describe the same appliance with a rounding difference. That is worth knowing before you reject a product over a unit mismatch.

The short answer

Treat a unit mismatch as a rounding issue, not a red flag, and decide on flat area and basket count instead. A quart total tells you less than one concrete fit claim.

What the two units actually mean

A US liquid quart is about 0.946 litres, so quarts are the larger unit. That means a number in quarts converts to a smaller number in litres for the same volume. If a listing shows the same digit in both fields, one of the two figures has almost certainly been carried across without conversion.

Where this listing does it

The dual-basket Chefman states 6 quarts in its title and its Size field, and 6 liters in the Capacity field. Six litres is roughly 6.3 US quarts, so the Capacity field describes a slightly larger cavity than the title does. Both figures are printed on this site, and the discrepancy is flagged as FLG-034.

Does a rounding gap change the purchase?

Almost never on its own. A 6.3-quart cavity and a 6-quart cavity hold the same dinner. What does change the purchase is how that volume is split: two 3-quart baskets cannot take a single large item, even though their total is the same as one 6-quart basket.

The number to check instead

The listing's own concrete claims are more useful than either unit figure. The Emeril listing states it fits a 12-inch pizza and can rotisserie a 6-lb chicken. Those are statements about usable interior size, and you can measure a pizza pan against them.

What to do at checkout

If a capacity figure is the deciding factor for you, check the live listing, because fields change. If the two units still disagree, the smaller figure is the safe planning number.
