A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Capacity is one clue, but a volume label alone does not show the shape of the cooking area or how your usual food will fit. Check the interior layout, maker's fit examples, and the appliance's outside dimensions before deciding what the number means for your kitchen.

The short answer

Write down the largest item you expect to cook, how many different foods you prepare at once, and your usable counter space. Compare those needs with the maker's listed dimensions and layout.

1. Total versus usable

Six quarts split into two three-quart baskets is not the same as one six-quart basket. The split gives you two separate cooks; the single basket gives you one bigger flat area. Neither is better in the abstract — they suit different meals.

2. Flat area, not volume

A volume figure does not tell you the usable width or arrangement of a basket or tray. Look for interior dimensions or maker-stated fit examples; if the listing does not give them, leave the fit question open rather than inferring from the quart figure.

3. What the listing says fits

A model's listing may give a fit example. The current P-02 listing mentions a 12-inch pizza and rotisserie for a 6-pound chicken. Treat those as seller-stated examples, check the live listing and manual, and do not assume they describe every food or rack position.

4. Physical size on the counter

A bigger cavity usually means a bigger box. Compare dimensions before you talk yourself into capacity you do not have room for. The 26-quart oven is 16.9 inches deep; the 8-quart basket is 12.75.

5. Your household, honestly

Do not convert the label into a guessed number of diners. Write down a meal you cook often, the largest ingredient or dish, and whether you would rather cook in one area, separate zones, or batches.

6. Watch the units

The P-01 listing uses different units in its title and capacity field: 6 quarts and 6 liters. Both statements are retained in the record; this site does not decide which unit to prefer. See FLG-034.
