Air fryer at Thanksgiving: what actually fits

Planning a holiday meal? List the dishes that need oven space, then check which tasks a countertop appliance's current listing and manual actually support. These are planning prompts, not a claim about what is popular or a tested serving recommendation.

The short answer

Plan around verified dimensions and maker-stated fit examples. Confirm clearance, instructions, and any power details in the live listing and manual before the meal.

What a countertop oven can take off your hands

The current P-02 listing gives fit examples including a 12-inch pizza and a 6-pound rotisserie chicken. Check the current listing and manual before mapping those examples to a holiday dish; they are seller statements, not our capacity test.

Do not extrapolate a fit example

A listed 6-pound chicken fit does not confirm that a whole turkey or another dish will fit. Measure your food and check the usable interior and manual before planning a meal around it.

Check your batch plan

If you are considering the P-01 dual-basket layout, check the listing's separate-basket and timing claims against the number of dishes you want to cook. We have not tested how quickly a particular holiday side cooks in this unit.

Check power and setup
