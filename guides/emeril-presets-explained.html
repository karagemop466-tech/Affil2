Emeril 26QT: 24 or 25 presets, and why it matters less than fit

The Emeril 26QT listing states 24 one-touch presets in its title and 25 in its About this item section. Rather than pick a number, this guide explains what a preset is, why the listing disagrees with itself, and what to check before a count moves your decision.

The short answer

Do not decide on 24 or 25. Decide on whether the modes you actually use are good, and on whether a 16.9-inch-deep, 26-quart oven fits your counter. The preset count is the last thing worth arguing about.

A preset is not a function

A function is a cooking mode — air fry, bake, broil, roast. A preset is a saved temperature and time for one food. Twenty-four presets can still sit behind a handful of modes. The number on the panel is a convenience count, not a measure of how many things the machine can do.

Why the listing says two numbers

On this listing the count is printed as 24 in the title, the Product Description and the Configuration fields, and as 25 in the About this item section. Both figures come off the same page, so neither is chosen here — the disagreement is flagged as FLG-021 and printed rather than smoothed over.

What the count does not tell you

The modes behind the presets are air fry, bake, broil, dehydrate, roast, slow cook, toast and reheat, according to the listing. That is the list that matters. A preset count tells you how many saved buttons there are, not whether the modes you use are good.

Check fit before you check presets

Before the count, check whether the machine fits your kitchen and your cooking. The 26QT is 26 quarts (24.6 liters), 16.9 inches deep, 15.8 wide and 14.8 tall, rated 75–500 °F at 120 volts and 1700 watts, and the listing says it rotisseries a 6-pound chicken and bakes a 12-inch pizza. Its item weight is not stated on the listing (FLG-022).

When a listing contradicts itself

Where a listing prints two different numbers for the same thing, the manual is the tie-breaker, not the marketing copy. Treat the preset count the same way you would treat any other self-contradiction on the page.
