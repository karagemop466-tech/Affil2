How verification works

  1. Open the official page. For a product, that is the amazon.com/dp/ listing.
  2. Read the value in the section that carries it — title, About this item, or the product-information table.
  3. Record it, with the date and the URL.
  4. If two parts of the same page disagree, record both and raise a flag. Do not pick the flattering one.
  5. If nothing on the page carries the value, leave it blank and say so.

A note on the listings: an Amazon product page is not a stable document. Titles, bullet copy and the structured information table can change without notice. That is why every row here is dated and why the flag register keeps corrections rather than erasing them.
