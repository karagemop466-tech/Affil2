Open Questions

Everything that has to be answered before a decision here is actually a decision. Each row names what is missing, how to go and get it, what it blocks, and who realistically can supply it. None of these are answered by guessing, so the answer column says what we know: usually nothing.

What this page is for

Twenty questions, grouped by who can answer them. Most need the owner's account access or a yes/no decision. Six need a seller to clarify a listing that contradicts itself. One needs Amazon support. The rest are editorial choices that only the owner can make.

If you only answer one thing, answer Q-04 — whether the tagged links are being recorded. Everything about revenue depends on it, and nothing else does.

What we do know, and where it came from

  • The five short links resolve to five specific ASINs. Observed on 2026-11-20, and recorded separately from intent on each product page.
  • Every specification on this site was read off an official listing on 2026-11-20. The source register links every one.
  • The compliance rules are public and quoted. Amazon's Operating Agreement Section 5, Program Policies 2(a) and 2(b), FTC disclosure guidance and Google's outbound-link guidance are all linked with the exact wording used.
  • USDA publishes the minimum internal temperatures used in the thermometer guides, and that page is linked rather than paraphrased.

What we deliberately do not know

Search volume for any topic, conversion rate for any product, whether a given product is better than another, and what any of these appliances is like to actually use. Four of those require tools or access we do not have. The fourth requires buying the products, and this site does not pretend it did.
