Open Questions

Everything that has to be answered before a decision here is actually a decision. Each row names what is missing, how to go and get it, what it blocks, and who realistically can supply it. None of these are answered by guessing, so the answer column says what we know: usually nothing.

What this page is for

Fourteen questions, grouped by who can answer them. Six need the owner's account access or a yes/no decision. Four need a seller to clarify a listing that contradicts itself. Two need Amazon support. Two are editorial choices that only the owner can make.

If you only answer one thing, answer Q-04 — whether the tagged links are being recorded. Everything about revenue depends on it, and nothing else does.

What the records support

  • Short-link observations are recorded separately from direct listing references and intent. One supplied link (P-04) returned a checkpoint rather than a product page on the latest recorded check; do not treat the direct listing as proof of that redirect.
  • Published product facts link to the official listing used as their source on 2026-11-20. Values that were not in the fetched text stay blank, and conflicts stay flagged. See the source register.
  • The compliance rules are public and quoted. Amazon's Operating Agreement Section 5, Program Policies 2(a) and 2(b), FTC disclosure guidance and Google's outbound-link guidance are all linked with the exact wording used.
  • USDA publishes the minimum internal temperatures used in the thermometer guides, and that page is linked rather than paraphrased.

What we deliberately do not know

Four of those require tools or access we do not have. The fourth requires buying the products, and this site does not pretend it did.
